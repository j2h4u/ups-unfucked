<objective>
Reframe `evaluate_test_scheduling` from a sulfation/cycle-ROI decision engine into a simple
diagnostic capacity/SoH verification scheduler driven by a ~365-day (IEEE-1188) time cadence,
and fix the confirmed bootstrap deadlock (SCH-01, SCH-02, SCH-03).

Purpose: Today the scheduler only proposes a test when `sulfation_score`/`cycle_roi` exceed
thresholds, but those inputs are 0.0 until a discharge completes — and no discharge happens
without a proposed test. The result is a permanent deadlock where no first test is ever
scheduled. Making the persistent time cadence the *propose* driver kills the deadlock and
aligns the daemon with honest, diagnostic-only behavior (no autonomous "desulfation").

Output: A reframed pure scheduler function, updated SchedulerManager wiring, and rewritten
scheduler unit tests. This plan does NOT delete sulfation.py/cycle_roi.py (Wave 2) — it only
stops the scheduler from depending on them, so Wave 2 can delete cleanly with no live callers.
</objective>
