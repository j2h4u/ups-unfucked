<objective>
Remove all disproven-premise sulfation/cycle-ROI machinery: delete the two kernel modules,
strip their callers and persisted/exported fields, remove blackout-credit-as-desulfation and
recovery-delta-as-evidence logic, and delete or surgically clean the tests that exercised them
(RET-01, RET-02, RET-03, RET-04).

Purpose: The v3.0 active-desulfation premise is disproven (discharge forms sulfate; charging
reverses it; the daemon has no charge control). Carrying the scoring code, persisted history,
health/MOTD/journald fields, and blackout-credit machinery makes the daemon lie about what it
does. This plan makes the codebase honest. Wave 1 already removed the scheduler's dependency on
these symbols, so deletion leaves no live callers.

Output: Two deleted kernel files, a slimmed discharge pipeline + model + health/exporter/MOTD
surface, and a clean test suite. Final grep/vulture gates prove completeness.
</objective>
